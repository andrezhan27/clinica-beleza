support what a technique or product is; they do not establish the clinic's exact
protocol, equipment, product selection, regulatory status or included services.

promotional percentages or guarantees.

## Clinic facts and remaining uncertainty

Pack compositions, prices and published durations come from `data/pricing.ts`.
Component explanations are general; procedure order and intervals are individual.
Ambiguous counts, such as how massage is scheduled in Body Sculpt, are left for
the team to clarify rather than expanded into invented inclusions.

At the user's request, Lipo Gordura, Coolfit, Foto-biodinâmica and Peeling das
Estrelas remain general. Foto-biodinâmica is not assumed to mean LED
photobiomodulation, IPL or photodynamic therapy. Its English name is neutral.
No wavelength, injectable ingredient or device model is inferred.

Olhar Premium, the genetic-test panel, exosome products and surgery options also
avoid unconfirmed specifics. The guide uses Longevidade / Longevity and the
requested menopause wording, but no menopause service is invented.
